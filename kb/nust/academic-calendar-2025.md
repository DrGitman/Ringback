These are the confirmed dates from the 2025 institutional calendar, approved by Senate. Prefer this over a guess whenever a caller asks about a specific 2025 date, but if the calendar is later revised, the published calendar on the website is still the final authority.

### Semester dates

Semester 1 classes start 10 February and end 16 May for exam-based courses, or 06 June for CASS (continuous-assessment) courses, with a mid-semester break from 31 March to 04 April. The mid-year student recess runs 16 June to 11 July. Semester 2 classes start 14 July and end 10 October for exam-based courses, or 07 November for CASS courses, with a mid-semester break from 08 to 12 September. Academic activities for the year end 12 December.

### Examination sessions

Special/exit exams run 04–06 March (semester 1) and 18–22 August (semester 2). Supplementary/2nd-opportunity exams run 13–28 January, 07–18 July, and 01–11 December. Ordinary exams run 27 May–13 June (semester 1) and 20 October–07 November (semester 2).

### Graduation ceremonies

The main graduation ceremony runs 08–09 May (Thursday and Friday), with an additional ceremony at the Eenhana site on 15 May. The second annual graduation ceremony is 10 October.
